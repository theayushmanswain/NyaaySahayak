# NyaaySahayak – Requirements

## Functional Requirements
- The system shall allow users to upload or capture images of legal documents.
- The system shall extract text from printed or handwritten documents.
- The system shall simplify complex legal language into easy-to-understand terms.
- The system shall translate explanations into local Indian languages and dialects.
- The system shall provide voice-based explanations of documents.
- The system shall support voice-based follow-up questions.
- The system shall warn users that it provides legal information, not legal advice.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- The system shall work in low-bandwidth rural environments.
- The system shall ensure user data privacy and in-memory document processing.
- The system shall provide accurate and unbiased legal explanations.
- The system shall scale automatically with user demand using serverless architecture.

## Constraints
- The system must use AWS-managed AI services.
- The system must comply with responsible AI guidelines.
